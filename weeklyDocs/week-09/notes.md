## LinkedIn Rewrite

### Headline
The old headline described what you used to be. The new one describes what you are now:

```
AI Prompt Engineer | Internal Business Tools | Python · Anthropic API · LLM Systems
```

Short. Keyword-rich for LinkedIn search. Niche-specific (not just "AI Engineer").

### About Section (3 paragraphs)

**Paragraph 1 — Who you are:**
After 4 years away from tech (family first, no regrets), I've returned with a clear direction: AI Prompt Engineering. I'm specifically focused on internal business tools — helping companies use AI to process documents, summarize meetings, and surface knowledge from their existing content.

**Paragraph 2 — What you've built:**
Over the past 2 months I've built three documented projects: a customer message classifier, a meeting notes processor, and a document Q&A system. Each one has a tested prompt, working Python code, and an honest write-up of what failed and what I changed.

**Paragraph 3 — What you're looking for:**
Looking for an in-house prompt engineer role where I can design and iterate on AI systems that save real time for real teams. Based in Australia — open to remote.

## Portfolio Page

A simple Carrd or Notion page linking everything together:

- 3 project cards with one-sentence descriptions
- Link to GitHub
- Link to LinkedIn
- One paragraph bio (same as the LinkedIn About)
- Loom demo embed for the capstone

This page is what you put in every job application as "portfolio link."

---

## Resume

One page. Four sections:

1. **Contact** — name, location, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio URL
2. **Summary** — 2 sentences: what you are, what you build
3. **Projects** — the 3 portfolio projects with brief descriptions and measurable results
4. **Skills** — Python, Anthropic/OpenAI APIs, prompt engineering techniques, Git, JSON, system design

Previous tech experience (iOS/Swift) goes at the bottom as "Previous Experience" — it's evidence that you can build, not the headline.

## The Gap Question

You'll be asked about the 4-year gap. The answer is simple and true:

> "I stepped away from tech to focus on family. That's a decision I'd make again. I've spent the last two months re-entering with a focused direction — AI prompt engineering — and I have three documented projects to show for it. The gap is part of the story, not a hole in it."

That's it. No over-explaining. No apologizing.
