## 🎯 The Decision

**Chosen niche: Internal Business Tools**

Specifically: AI systems that help teams process documents, draft communications, and answer questions from internal knowledge — the unglamorous but high-value work that fills most corporate workdays.

---

## Why This Niche

**Market pull is strong.** Every company with an internal knowledge base, document library, or email-heavy workflow is a potential customer. This isn't a niche for one industry — it applies everywhere.

**The problems are well-defined.** "Summarise this report" and "draft a reply to this email" are clearer, more testable problems than "make our content more engaging." Clearer problems = better prompts = more demonstrable results.

**It connects to existing skills.** Having an iOS/Swift background means I understand how software integrates with user workflows. Internal tools are about workflow integration more than consumer UX flair.

**The competition is thinner.** Lots of people are building customer-facing AI. Fewer are focused on internal productivity — and it's where most AI Prompt Engineer job descriptions actually live.

---

## Niche Statement

> "I help companies use AI to process documents, draft internal communications, and surface knowledge from their existing content — saving hours of manual work every week."
