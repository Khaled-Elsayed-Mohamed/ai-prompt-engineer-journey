# 📖 Week 5 · Day 1 — 5 Job Postings Research

**AI Prompt Engineer Journey · Khaled**

> **Goal:** Read 5 real "AI Prompt Engineer" or "LLM Engineer" job postings carefully. What do companies actually want?

---

## The 5 Postings I Read

Searched on LinkedIn and Seek (Australian job boards). Mix of Australian companies and remote-friendly international roles.

---

## What Appeared in Every Posting

**Python skills.** Every single one mentioned Python. Not "nice to have" — listed alongside core requirements. Building in the playground isn't enough.

**API experience.** "Experience with OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar LLM APIs" appeared in all 5. This is table stakes.

**Portfolio or examples.** All 5 mentioned "demonstrable examples" or "portfolio of AI projects." Not implied — explicitly stated. This validates the whole GitHub documentation approach.

**Cross-functional communication.** Repeatedly described as working with "product teams," "data teams," or "business stakeholders." This isn't a pure engineering role — you need to translate between technical and non-technical people.

---

## What Separated Mid-Level from Senior

**Mid-level postings** focused on: writing prompts, iterating based on feedback, testing outputs.

**Senior postings** added: evaluation frameworks, prompt optimization at scale, training data curation, and "owning the end-to-end AI system design."

The difference is ownership — mid-level executes, senior designs the system.

---

## Niche Signals

Looking at the actual job descriptions for the work they want done:
- 3/5 mentioned document processing, summarization, or knowledge extraction
- 2/5 mentioned customer support automation
- 1/5 was explicitly about content generation

Internal business tools showed up most. That's consistent with where enterprise AI spend is going.

---

## One Line Summary Per Posting

1. **FinTech company (Sydney):** "Design and optimize prompts for document analysis workflows in a financial reporting context." — Internal tools, high precision required.

2. **Enterprise SaaS (remote):** "Own the prompt layer for our AI assistant, used internally by 500+ employees." — Internal tools, scale matters.

3. **Marketing agency (Melbourne):** "Build prompt templates for content generation across social, email, and web." — Content niche, less my interest.

4. **Healthcare startup (remote):** "Design prompts for clinical note summarization and patient communication drafts." — Internal tools with high-stakes requirements.

5. **Consulting firm (Sydney):** "Develop AI-powered tools to help consultants draft proposals and analyze client documents." — Exactly the internal tools niche.

---

## Conclusion

The job market is confirming what the niche research suggested: internal business tools are where most of the actual hiring is happening. This niche is the right choice.
