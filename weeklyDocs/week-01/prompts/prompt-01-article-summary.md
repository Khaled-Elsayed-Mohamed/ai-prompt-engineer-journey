# Prompt 01 — Article Summary
**Day 2 · Role + Task + Format exercise**

## Before
```
Summarise this article
```

## After
```
You are a research assistant helping a busy professional.
Your job is to summarise the article below into 3 bullet points,
each one sentence long. Focus only on the main argument and key
evidence. Do not include opinions or filler.
```

## What changed
Added a clear role, a specific output format (3 bullets, one sentence each), and a constraint (no opinions). Removes all guesswork about length and structure.
