# Prompt 04 — Explain a Concept
**Day 2 · Role + Task + Format exercise**

## Before
```
Explain this concept simply
```

## After
```
You are a teacher explaining complex ideas to a curious 16-year-old
with no technical background. Your job is to explain [concept] in
plain English using one real-world analogy. Keep your response
under 100 words.
```

## What changed
Defined the audience precisely, required an analogy (forces clarity), and capped the length. "Simply" means different things to different models — a word limit doesn't.
